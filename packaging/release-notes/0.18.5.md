### What's new in 0.18.5

- **Choose a balanced or aggressive Advertising policy.** Advertising remains the recommended lower-breakage tier; Advertising — Aggressive is an explicit opt-in superset for broader hostname coverage. The two tiers never stack.
- **Replace tiers as one durable policy change.** Enabling either tier atomically disables the other, carries first-switch exceptions forward without overwriting either tier's saved state, and publishes only after SQLite commits the complete change. Corrupt dual-enabled state disables both and requires a fresh review.
- **Review substantially broader, evidence-backed coverage.** Deterministic offline generation derives 42,384 balanced and 42,433 aggressive destinations from immutable EasyList and HaGeZi snapshots, with protected product surfaces, conservative syntax handling, stable entry identities, and Public Suffix List boundaries.
- **Cover PubADX without overclaiming.** Balanced blocks the independently observed `platform.pubadx.one` and `imp9.pubadx.one` hosts exactly; Aggressive covers the `pubadx.one` suffix. `pubadx.com` remains reachable, and Flowlight makes no ownership or affiliation claim between the domains.
- **Keep hostname blocking honest.** Policies explains that Flowlight refuses every connection to a matched host but cannot remove same-origin YouTube or Meta ads, browser overlays, layout gaps, or path-specific resources on shared hosts.
- **Make every generated decision reviewable.** The editor exposes signed catalog sequence and digest, pack version, content digest, license, complete destinations, and content-addressed provenance. Vendored source locks retain upstream revisions, byte counts, hashes, notices, and license texts.
- **Bound the larger catalog end to end.** The signed catalog remains format v1 with a measured 16 MiB transport cap and 32 MiB extension-transfer cap. Fail-capable tests cover source drift, unsafe suffixes, tier superset, deterministic output, atomic persistence, stable event provenance, restart behavior, and full-catalog decode, matcher, encoding, and IPC budgets.

macOS 15 or later, Apple silicon and Intel.
