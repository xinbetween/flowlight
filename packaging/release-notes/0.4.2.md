### What's new in 0.4.2

**Bluetooth: which devices are paired, which are connected, and when that changed.**

A Mac sends and receives over more than TCP and UDP, and Flowlight was blind to the rest. The new **Devices** screen starts to fix that.

- **What's paired and what's connected**, read from the system every half minute — the device's name, what it is, who makes it, and its address. Connecting and disconnecting are recorded, so there's a history of when the headphones arrived and when they left.
- **Which applications are built to use Bluetooth**, from each app's own `Info.plist`.
- **It's off until you turn it on.** Not because it needs a permission Flowlight doesn't already have — it doesn't — but because watching more of your Mac should be your decision rather than something an update did.

**What it can't tell you, and says so rather than implying otherwise.** There are no byte counts here. macOS keeps no per-app accounting for Bluetooth, and the byte-level HCI traces that would give one need Apple's PacketLogger profile, which an ordinary app cannot read. The app list is *who asked* for Bluetooth, not *who was granted* it: the grants live in a database an app can't open.

USB and external storage are next.

### Install

Signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

- `brew install --cask xinbetween/tap/flowlight`
- `Flowlight.dmg` — drag to Applications.
- `Flowlight-0.4.2.pkg` — installs to /Applications; offers to quit a running copy first.

macOS 15 or later, Apple silicon and Intel.
