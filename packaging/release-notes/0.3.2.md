### What's new in 0.3.2

**Export to OpenTelemetry or a SIEM** — so what Flowlight records isn't stranded in a local file.

This is the first thing Flowlight does that deliberately sends recorded traffic off the machine, against a product whose pitch is "no account, no cloud, no telemetry". So it is built to be predictable rather than convenient:

- **Off until you configure it, and there is no Flowlight service to send to.** You name the endpoint: your own collector, your own SIEM.
- **OTLP over HTTP** with a JSON body — rollups to `/v1/metrics`, alerts to `/v1/logs`, because an alert is an event with a sentence attached and a metric would lose the sentence. Newline-delimited JSON is there for collectors that just want lines.
- **The panel lists every field that leaves**, and previews a real payload built from your own data before anything is sent. You can know what your SIEM will see before turning it on.
- **Nothing from HTTPS inspection can be exported.** Not by policy — by shape. The payload types carry rollups and alerts only, so there is nowhere to put a header, a body or a tool call even by accident, and a test fails if an undeclared field ever appears.
- **Auth headers live in your login Keychain**, not in preferences, and only their names are ever displayed.
- **What Flowlight sends appears in Flowlight**, in Live and Reports, like any other app's traffic — including a collector on `127.0.0.1`, which is the common case and was the one that nearly got hidden.

Two things to expect. Rollups are folded about two minutes behind, so an interval under a minute polls more often without data arriving sooner. And **Test connection** proves the endpoint answers, not that it accepted your credentials — many collectors take an empty request without checking auth, so the "last successful export" line is the signal worth trusting.

### Install

Signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

- `brew install --cask xinbetween/tap/flowlight`
- `Flowlight.dmg` — drag to Applications.
- `Flowlight-0.3.2.pkg` — installs to /Applications; offers to quit a running copy first.

macOS 15 or later, Apple silicon and Intel.
