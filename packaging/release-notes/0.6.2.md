### What's new in 0.6.2

**Flowlight speaks ten languages.**

English, 简体中文, 繁體中文, 日本語, 한국어, Français, Deutsch, Italiano, Español and Português.

- **It follows your Mac by default.** If your preferred languages include one Flowlight speaks, that is what it uses. For Chinese it tells Simplified from Traditional by script and region, so a Mac set to `zh-TW` or `zh-HK` gets Traditional rather than Simplified — the kind of mistake people notice immediately. A regional variant still finds its language: `fr-CA` gets French, `pt-BR` gets Portuguese.
- **Or choose one in Settings ⌘,** under General. It changes immediately rather than on next launch, because a language picker that needs a restart to show its work is one people assume is broken. When the choice is *System*, the row says underneath which language that actually turned out to be.
- **Anything untranslated falls back to English**, not to a blank or a dotted identifier. That is what keeps a partly-translated screen readable rather than broken.

This release covers the navigation, the settings and the common controls — the words on screen no matter which screen you are on. The longer explanatory passages follow next.

### Install

Signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

- `brew install --cask xinbetween/tap/flowlight`
- `Flowlight.dmg` — drag to Applications.
- `Flowlight-0.6.2.pkg` — installs to /Applications; offers to quit a running copy first.

macOS 15 or later, Apple silicon and Intel.
