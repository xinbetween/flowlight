### What's new in 0.18.4

- **Show Connections loading again.** The Flowlight mark restores its lightweight flip while data and final graph geometry load. It stays still with Reduce Motion and in deterministic screenshot fixtures.
- **Draw routes as one smooth movement.** Difficult obstacle detours keep continuous tangents without the long horizontal or vertical shelves that made paths look like rounded rectangles, while preserving card clearance and bounded routing work.
- **Let traffic volume read at a glance.** Observed ribbons scale monotonically from KB through MB and GB with bounded logarithmic width. Flow-only evidence remains visible, relationship-only support edges stay minimal, and only terminal observed edges own counted traffic.
- **Make inspection setup stay out of the way.** Advanced, mock, request-modification, and response-modification disclosure labels toggle across their full row. Explicit HTTPS inspection enablement stays on across relaunches until you turn it off; optional automatic timers remain available.
- **Open Policies as a compact overview.** Network rules, Agent allowlists, and Managed packs start folded independently while retaining their status summaries and full-row controls.
- **Keep public installation choices truthful.** The homepage keeps the Developer download, GitHub, and Homebrew paths while temporarily withholding Mac App Store promotion until Apple approves the intended current Store version.
- **Keep release evidence canonical.** Signed preparation and its retained-artifact verifier share the same required-check contract. The successful workflow-bound CodeQL gate remains mandatory while GitHub's redundant neutral status is ignored, with exact workflow, pull request, reviewer, and commit provenance retained.

### Install

Signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

- `brew install --cask xinbetween/tap/flowlight`
- `Flowlight.dmg` — drag to Applications.
- `Flowlight-0.18.4.pkg` — installs to /Applications; offers to quit a running copy first.

macOS 15 or later, Apple silicon and Intel.

Full changelog: https://github.com/xinbetween/flowlight/releases
