### What's new in 0.2.8

- **Worth a look, in Reports.** A new view that compares the apps in the report with each other and points at the ones whose destinations don't look like the rest: talking to far more places than its peers, addresses that never resolved to a name, hostnames that look generated rather than chosen, sending much more than it receives, and protocols that are a sideline rather than the app's purpose. Each finding shows the evidence behind it, and clicking one narrows the report to that app.

  It's called *Worth a look*, not *Suspicious*, on purpose. What the data supports is "unusual compared with the other apps here" — a backup tool talks to a lot of places for good reasons. The panel says so rather than implying a verdict it can't reach.

- **Two corrections to that analysis before it shipped.** A row records every protocol seen on it against a single byte total, and charging that total to each one made an app's protocol shares add up to more than its traffic — enough to hide a real finding behind a category the app hadn't earned. And the panel drew on the rows already loaded for the table, which stop at ten thousand; an app past that cap was silently never considered, on exactly the busy machines where it would matter most. It now asks for every app's destinations on their own.

- **A tidier Alerts toolbar.** A rule picker, a checkbox and two buttons crowded into one row have become two menus: one for what's listed, one for marking things seen.

- **The website now matches the app.** *Worth a look*, running in the background, the Capture view and searching inside bodies are all documented, and the count of recognised LLM providers is right (24, not 25).

### Install

Signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

- `brew install --cask xinbetween/tap/flowlight`
- `Flowlight.dmg` — drag to Applications.
- `Flowlight-0.2.8.pkg` — installs to /Applications; offers to quit a running copy first.

macOS 15 or later, Apple silicon and Intel.
