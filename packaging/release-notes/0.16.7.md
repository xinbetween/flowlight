### What's new in 0.16.7

- **Keep large maps calm.** Stable barycentric sweeps reduce avoidable relationship crossings, while topology-aware horizontal and vertical spacing keeps every semantic stage and card inside the canvas.
- **Update traffic without moving the map.** Node frames, ports, and curves are cached by stable topology and viewport size; changing byte and connection totals refreshes ribbon widths without recomputing geometry.
- **Draw only what is on screen.** Zooming and panning derive an exact graph-space viewport and skip offscreen ribbons and particles while keeping every card and relationship control available to keyboard and VoiceOver.
- **Scale with measured evidence.** A 600-relationship benchmark guards layout cost alongside collision, route-avoidance, hit-testing, Fit, zoom, Reduce Motion, and accessibility regressions.

Signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

- `Flowlight.dmg` — drag to Applications.

macOS 15 or later, Apple silicon and Intel.
