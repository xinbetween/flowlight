### What's new in 0.4.3

**USB and external storage, and the end of the channels milestone.**

Flowlight now watches all three channels it set out to: the network, peer-to-peer Wi-Fi, and the devices attached to the Mac.

- **What's plugged in**, on the Devices screen: the device's name, what it is, who makes it, and a drive's capacity. Hubs and controllers are walked through rather than listed, so you see what you attached and not the handful of controllers every Mac has always had.
- **Volumes appear the moment they mount.** USB devices are read periodically, but a drive being plugged in or ejected arrives immediately — that is the event people actually care about, and it shouldn't wait half a minute.
- **A history of arrivals and departures**, alongside Bluetooth's, so "when was that drive last connected?" has an answer.
- **Off until you turn it on**, like Bluetooth.

**No throughput**, for the same reason Bluetooth has no byte counts: macOS keeps no per-app accounting for these channels, and Flowlight would rather say so than show a number it made up.

### Install

Signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

- `brew install --cask xinbetween/tap/flowlight`
- `Flowlight.dmg` — drag to Applications.
- `Flowlight-0.4.3.pkg` — installs to /Applications; offers to quit a running copy first.

macOS 15 or later, Apple silicon and Intel.
